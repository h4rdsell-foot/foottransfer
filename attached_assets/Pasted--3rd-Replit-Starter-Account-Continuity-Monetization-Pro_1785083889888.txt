# 3rd Replit Starter Account – Continuity + Monetization Prompt

## Mission

You are continuing the build of a **premium, mobile-first marketplace and provider operating system** for:

- mobile foot technicians  
- mobile foot spa providers  
- pedicure specialists  
- in-home foot wellness professionals  

The project is already connected to a Git repository and **has core documentation** in `docs/` plus a master spec file. Your job is to:

- Respect all existing architecture and notes.

- Keep the build on a path that real providers will happily pay for.
- Maintain portability across all future environments (other Replit accounts, local, other AI agents, Railway, etc.).

Git is the source of truth. Replit is just the current workbench.

## Step 2 – Monetization rules (non‑negotiable)

This platform must ship with real revenue levers that providers in this niche will recognize and pay for.

### 1. Commission on bookings

- Implement a commission-based model:
  - Platform earns a % on each **completed booking** (target 10–15%).
- Commission must be visible in:
  - booking records,
  - provider earnings views,
  - payout summaries.

### 2. Subscription tiers for providers

Create **tiered plans** and wire them into the data model and UI:

- **Free**
  - Basic listing.
  - Limited bookings per month.
  - Standard commission rate.

- **Pro**
  - Better search/listing placement.
  - Repeat-client tools (rebook prompts, simple CRM).
  - Lower commission or better payout.
  - Basic analytics (bookings/week, earnings trend).

- **Premium**
  - Featured placement options.
  - Advanced analytics (retention, top services, zones).
  - Priority support & faster verification.
  - Best commission terms.

Subscription tier must control:
- what features providers see,
- what price/commission they pay,
- where they appear in the marketplace.

### 3. Featured listings

- Add a way for providers to pay for **featured** spots:
  - “Top in city”, “Featured provider”, homepage tiles.
- Admin can configure featured flags.
- Public pages should visually differentiate featured providers.

### 4. Lead credits (future-ready)

- Add hooks so providers can **buy credits for leads** later:
  - credit balance field,
  - basic UI placeholder in provider dashboard.
- You do not need a full lead-routing engine yet, just a data/UI path.

### 5. Transaction / payout fee hooks

- Support small flat fees on payouts (e.g. $1 per withdrawal).
- Show these transparently in provider earnings and payout views.

## Step 3 – Provider value rules

Every feature you add must answer:

> “Which provider pain am I removing?”

Must-have provider-facing value:

- **Service menu control**
  - Services with duration, price, add-ons, and travel fees.

- **Schedule & recurring visits**
  - Availability rules and recurring bookings for routine foot care.

- **Travel zones & buffers**
  - Radius/zone controls plus buffer times between visits.

- **Client CRM**
  - Repeat clients, history, notes, preferences.
  - “Due for rebook” list.

- **Reviews & trust badges**
  - Ratings, written reviews.
  - Verification badges (e.g. verified, senior-friendly, mobile specialist).

- **Earnings & payouts**
  - Clear overview of income, commission, subscription fees, payout status.

- **Simple marketing levers**
  - Featured listing toggle (when allowed by plan).
  - Simple promotions or rebooking prompts.

If a feature does not clearly improve one of these, it should not be prioritized now.

---

## Step 4 – Premium UX rules (funded startup feel)

The app must feel like a funded startup: **premium, minimal, mobile-first, trust-first**.

- Dashboards:
  - Top section: 3–5 key metrics + actions.
  - Below: lists, details, timelines, and feeds.

- Mobile-first:
  - All primary flows must work on small screens.
  - Avoid hover-only interactions for core actions.

- Trust-first:
  - Provider verification status is obvious.
  - Booking status is clearly labeled (requested, confirmed, en route, in service, completed).
  - “Who is coming” is clear: name, photo, credentials, ratings.

- Use:
  - branded header, status board, progress timeline, activity feed  
  only where they **increase clarity and trust**, not as decoration.

## Step 5 – Early launch & income focus

Keep the build focused on **month‑one viability**, not speculative features.

Priority outcomes:

1. Provider onboarding that works end‑to‑end:
   - Apply → submit docs → admin review → approval → live listing.

2. Booking → payment → review loop:
   - Client can search, book, pay, get the visit, and leave a review.

3. Provider income visibility:
   - Providers see bookings, earnings, commissions, subscription status, and payouts.

4. At least one monetization lever live:
   - Commission **or** Pro subscriptions must be wired into the product and visible in the UI.

Do not build deep extras until these outcomes are real.
